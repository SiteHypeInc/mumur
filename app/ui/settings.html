Get started

Hold your dictation key, speak, let go. Murmur types clean, punctuated text wherever your cursor is.

  1. 1 · Allow Murmur to listen and type

    macOS asks for three permissions: Microphone (on first dictation), Accessibility (to paste), and Input Monitoring (for the hotkey). Restart Murmur after granting Input Monitoring.

    No extra permissions needed on this platform.

  2. 2 · Pick your engine

    Keys are stored in your system keychain and sent only to the provider. Groq has a free tier for transcription.

  3. 3 · Test it

    Then click into any text box and hold Fn.
