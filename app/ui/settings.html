General

Engines

Speech to text

Cleanup

No key? Murmur still removes "um"s and stutters and fixes capitalization on its own. For free AI cleanup, install Ollama, run ollama pull llama3.2, and pick Ollama above.

Styles

Murmur detects what kind of app you're in and writes in the style you pick for it.

Styles are off while "Exact words only" is on (Engines → Cleanup). Turn that off to let Murmur reword in these tones.

App modes

Give specific apps or sites their own instructions, style, or auto-send. Sites win over apps.

Dictionary

Words Murmur should always spell your way

Names, products, jargon. Near-misses snap to these (“Shinade” → “Sinéad”, “charge bee” → “ChargeBee”).

Replacements

Say a phrase, get exact text. Great for emails, links and sign-offs.
